## Verdict: DO NOT RELEASE

Three must-fix items, all small. Everything else holds: the five designs and the chooser keep the brief's rules in every block I checked, the figures match the live files, stale, old and missing files are said per block, `/place/` and home are byte for byte unchanged, and the folding works by keyboard.

### Must fix

- **M1. `/observations/`: the silent-stations fold prints raw URLs instead of the towns.** Where a silent station is the nearest to a town, the fold reads "Braga (CIM) (nearest to ,,http://localhost:8811/place/?id=1030300,)" (seven such stations at the 17:41 UTC read, among them Braga (CIM), Leiria, Portimão (Praia da Rocha) and Santa Maria (Aeródromo)); the town names are lost and the page shows a URL as text. Cause: `silentFold()` in `src/assets/js/pages/observations.js` line 171 nests arrays three deep, and `h()` flattens one level only (the proposal's lesson 0.2). Fix: flatten fully, for example build the names with `flatMap` that returns flat nodes (`[' (nearest to ', ...towns.flatMap(...), ')']`), or call `h('p', {}, names.flat(Infinity), '.')`. Shot: `shots/observations-390-fold-open.jpg`.
- **M2. Postcard front: figures with no source, publisher or read time beside them on the visible side.** The front's message and the four "On the back" facts give the station reading (18.6 °C at 17:00 UTC), sea (up to 4 m), UV (0.8) and fire risk (class 1), but their source lines are on the back, which is `hidden` until the card is turned. The status band gives the six files' state and the postmark gives the warnings file's read time only. The brief: "Beside every figure: the source, the publisher and the time read." (A stale or missing file is said in the fact, by `agedClause` and `FAILED`, so this is about attribution, not staleness.) Fix, smallest: under the facts list, one line such as "IPMA, via data.promptugal.news: stations read 17:41 UTC, sea 17:41, UV 17:41, fire risk 17:40 · source lines on the back", built from `sourceState()` of the four files, with state words when not fresh. The message paragraph then has every source on the same side (warnings and forecast provLines are already on the front).
- **M3. A person is named on the public site.** `team/rounds/2/design/PROPOSAL.md` line 236 cites "the Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009) matrices", and behind the scenes publishes it (`_site/behind-the-scenes/rounds/2/design/PROPOSAL/index.html` and `.md`). The brief: "name no person"; this review was asked to accept only Dinis Cruz as the person who instructs. Fix: cite the work without its authors' names, for example "the 2009 colour-vision simulation matrices published in IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics 15(6)". The designer owns the file.

### Should fix

- **S1. Timetable on a phone: an item from a stale or old file has only a coloured left rule** (`tt-mark-stale`), no word. The band at the top and the "Sources and read times" list say it in words, so this is not hidden, but colour alone is the only sign at the item. Add the state word (for example "Sea (stale): total sea 2 to 4 m").
- **S2. Timetable at 1366: the "now" line runs through the text of Today's cells** (for example through "from 13:15" in the warnings row; D's open item 4). Draw it as a tick at the top of each cell, or lighter, behind the text.
- **S3. Timetable's row labels say "read 17:41 Portugal time"** while the provLine under the same row says "read 18 Nov, 17:41 UTC". Both are right (the label converts to Lisbon time, the same in winter), but two zones for one read time on one row invites doubt; use UTC in the label as the proposal says ("read <HH:MM> UTC").
- **S4. `/observations/` column button changes both its label and `aria-pressed`** ("Show all 9 columns", pressed false, to "Show 4 columns", pressed true): a screen reader hears "Show 4 columns, pressed" when four are not shown. Keep one label ("All 9 columns") with `aria-pressed`, or keep the changing label and drop `aria-pressed`. As specified in the proposal, so the designer's call.
- **S5. Two towns at 390 px is long**: 9,383 px with three towns (Large print 9,085 px, Workbench 7,992 px). No target was set for the designs; folding IPMA's texts and the repeated MeteoAlarm note per town (C's open items) would help.
- **S6. The forecast cards' summary at 390 reads "Bragança · today 9.8 to 16° · rain 100%area Bragança"** to a screen reader (the area span follows with no space or separator). Add " · " before "area".
