Forecasts by place

IPMA's daily forecast for 35 places, up to five days ahead: the lowest and highest temperature, the chance of rain, the weather type and the wind. Every place is drawn on the same temperature scale, so you can compare them at a glance.

When the collector read IPMA

The forecasts are read from IPMA by PrompTugal.news's collector. Each mark is one of its runs; a run that found new bytes from IPMA is marked as new data.
