UV index by place and day

IPMA's forecast of the UV index, the strength of the sun's ultraviolet radiation, for each place and day. Each cell shows IPMA's value and is coloured by its band, with the edges IPMA applies to decimal values: from Low (IPMA: below 3) to Extreme (IPMA: 11 and above). The band names are the World Health Organization's, which gives the ranges in whole numbers (Low is 0 to 2).

When the collector read IPMA

The UV forecast is read from IPMA by PrompTugal.news's collector. Each mark is one of its runs; a run that found new bytes from IPMA is marked as new data.
